# Community conduct

Keep issues, reviews and discussions respectful and specific. Critique code
and behaviour, give reproducible evidence, and welcome contributors of different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, spam and disclosure of another
person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close discussions or restrict participation when needed, and should
explain decisions without repeating private information.

For a conduct concern, ask the maintainer for a private contact without posting
the affected person's details publicly. For vulnerabilities, use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). A dedicated confidential conduct channel has not been
announced.
